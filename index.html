A letter for bby 💌

Take your time here. This page is made to feel like a little handwritten note discovered inside a storybook.

To my favorite little dreamer,

Hii bby,

if i could decorate one tiny corner of the world for you, i'd put warm lanterns in the sky, little flowers along every path, and fairy lights wherever the evening feels a bit too quiet. i think some moments deserve to be made softer and prettier, just because.

i hope you always keep that spark that makes you excited about the things you love. i hope you get to see places you've been dreaming about, laugh until your cheeks hurt, collect memories that feel like sunshine, and find little surprises in ordinary days.

this website is just a small collection of lights, silly hidden things, pictures, and words. wander around, tap everything that looks interesting, and let the lanterns do their thing. whenever you need a little reminder that good things still exist, come back to this tiny kingdom.

no grand reason, no special occasion needed—just thought you deserved a little magic today. 🌷
